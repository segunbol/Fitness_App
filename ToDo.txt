- How do I construct the process?
19/01/2025
    - There are 3 types of transaction for this app:
        - Subscription          = Income
        - Gym items Sales       = Income
        - Gym purchase of items = Expense
            - Expense is further broken down to Capital Expense & Recurring Expense
            - We will categorise expenses based on category (admin, salary, rent, etc)
    - Operations to be carried out on transactions:
        - Sum up total Subscription - Monthly and Annual basis
        - Sum up total item Sales -   Monthly and Annual basis
            - Sum up total paid items
            - Sum up total unpaid items
        - Sum up total Income( Subscription + Income) - Monthly and Annual basis
        - Sum up total Recurring Expense - Monthly and Annual basis
        - Sum up total Capital Expense - Monthly and Annual basis
            - we are including depreciciation (straight line method)
        - Sum up total expense (Recurring & Capital) - Monthly and Annual basis
        - Calculate the depreciation of Assets with straightline method;
            - update the used up construct
            - update the total depreciation
            - update the current year depreciation - this will be used in computing the annual fixed cost
    - Summary Component:
        Frontend:-
            - Show the financial position of the business, Total Income less Total Expense(All time, Beginning of Year till Date and Monthly)
                There will be 3 slides
            - Charts - Month on Month (total expense, total income, profit/loss)
                       Year on Year (total expense, total income, profit/loss)
            - Subscription amount based on Months, Biannual, Yearly
            - Button to click list of active and inactive customers
        Backend:- 
            - Compute data for Profit/loss(All time, Beginning of Year till Date and Monthly)
